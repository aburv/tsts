Community

Meet the contributors behind the court

Three developers share why they started contributing and what they want to build next.

04 September 2026Takbuff community5 min read

Open source projects are made of code, but they grow through the people who keep asking better questions. This month we spoke with three contributors who found Takbuff at very different points in their developer journeys.

Small contributions add up

Anu started with a copy edit, Meera opened a bug after a weekend match, and Rishi began by improving a loading state. Their first contributions were small by design, which made it easier to learn the project without needing to understand everything at once.

“A good first issue gave me a way into the product, not just the repository.” — Meera, contributor

Since then, those first steps have become feature work, design discussions, and thoughtful reviews for new contributors. The project is stronger because each person brought a different kind of attention to it.

Bring your perspective

You do not need to be an expert in the whole codebase to help. Try the app, describe what felt unclear, improve one small detail, or ask a question in an issue. The next useful contribution may begin with exactly that.
