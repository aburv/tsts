Maintainer notes

What changed in our first open beta

A practical look at the feedback loop behind this month's small, high-impact fixes.

11 September 2026Gurunathan Ramalingam4 min read

Opening Takbuff to more players changed the rhythm of development. Instead of guessing which parts mattered most, we had real games, real pauses, and real questions to work from.

Three signals stood out

Players consistently asked for clearer score recovery, faster access to recent matches, and fewer moments where the interface made them stop and think. Those signals became our first beta priorities.

Our rule for the beta: every fix should make the next game easier to start, understand, or finish.

We tightened loading states, made empty screens more useful, and moved the most common actions closer to a player's thumb. None of these changes are flashy. Together, they make the product feel more dependable.

What comes next

The beta is now a conversation rather than a checkpoint. We will keep publishing the reasoning behind changes so contributors can review the direction, challenge assumptions, and help us choose the next improvement.
