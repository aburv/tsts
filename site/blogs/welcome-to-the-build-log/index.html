Welcome to the Takbuff build log

Why we are building the project in the open and how you can help shape what comes next.

28 June 2026Aburv team3 min read

Welcome to the Takbuff build log. This is where we will share the thinking behind new features, the lessons hidden in bugs, and the contributions that make the project better.

Why write in public?

Building in the open gives a feature more than a final announcement. It gives people a chance to understand the problem, question the approach, and offer a better idea before the work is finished.

You do not need an account or a title to take part. Play, notice, ask, and contribute in whatever form fits you.

Some posts will be technical. Others will be about the game, the community, or the decisions that happen between a rough idea and a shipped feature. We will keep them honest, useful, and connected to the work.

What to expect here

  • Monthly notes from maintainers and contributors.
  • Feature stories that explain the decisions behind the interface.
  • Practical ways to find an issue and join the project.

Thanks for being here at the start. The best version of this log will be written with you.
