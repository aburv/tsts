01 / First steps

Getting started

What is Takbuff?

Takbuff is a digital home for sepak takraw. Explore the game, follow its stories, and use the app to keep track of the ways you play.

How do I get started?

Visit the Takbuff app, create an account with Google Sign-In, and follow the prompts. You can also browse the game guide without an account.

Do I need an account to use Takbuff?

No. You can read the guides and learn the basics without signing in. An account is helpful when a feature needs to save your preferences or sync your activity.

02 / Read the court

The game

What is sepak takraw?

Sepak takraw is a fast-paced sport played over a net with a woven ball. Players use their feet, legs, torso, and head to keep the ball in the air; hands are off-limits.

How many players are on a team?

It depends on the format. Regu is played with three players, doubles with two, and quad with four. Each format gives the rally a different rhythm.

How does scoring work?

Every rally earns a point. A set is usually won by the first side to reach 15 points with a two-point advantage, subject to the match rules in use.

03 / We are listening

Account & support

How do I change or delete my account?

Send us a request through the contact page using the email connected to your account. We may ask you to verify ownership before making changes.

I found a bug. Where should I report it?

Open a bug report on GitHub with what happened, what you expected, and the device or browser you were using.

How can I suggest a new feature?

We would love to hear it. Share the idea in a feature request, including the problem it would solve for players.
