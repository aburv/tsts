Find your balance

The quiet skill behind the loudest moments on court: staying ready before the ball arrives.

June 2025Takbuff team4 min read

Balance in sepak takraw is not standing still. It is the ability to arrive at the next touch with enough control to choose what happens next. That control starts before the ball reaches you.

Ready is a moving position

After every kick, recover toward the middle of the play. Keep your weight where you can move it quickly, and resist the urge to admire a good touch. The next decision is already on its way.

Balance is a promise to your future self: I will leave enough room to make the next play.

In practice, try finishing each touch with both feet ready to move. Ask a partner to send the ball just outside your comfort zone, then focus on the recovery rather than the perfect return. Stability is built in the moment after the mistake.

Make space for your teammates

A balanced player also makes the court feel larger for everyone else. Call early, cover the open lane, and trust a teammate to take the ball when it is theirs. Good positioning is generosity with a little geometry.

When the whole team stays available, the spectacular moments become easier. Balance gives skill somewhere to land.
