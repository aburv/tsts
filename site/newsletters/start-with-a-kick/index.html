Start with a kick

A beginner’s field guide to the game, the court, and the first point that keeps you coming back.

April 2025Takbuff team4 min read

Sepak takraw looks spectacular from the outside. Feet reach where hands usually would, the ball moves quickly, and a single point can turn into a small piece of theatre. Starting to play is much simpler than it looks.

Begin with three habits

Keep your eyes on the ball, stay light on your feet, and give yourself room to make mistakes. These habits matter more than a perfect kick. They keep the first session curious instead of tense.

Your first goal is not a highlight. It is one clean touch that gives the next player something useful.

Find a comfortable stance and practice lifting the ball with the inside of your foot. Let the ball travel only as high as you can comfortably follow. Repeat the same movement until it starts to feel familiar, then add a partner.

The first rally

When you play with someone else, count successful touches instead of points. A rally of five is a win. A rally of ten is a conversation. Call for the ball, celebrate the save, and reset without making the last mistake bigger than it was.

The game rewards attention quickly. Start small, keep the ball moving, and let the more dramatic skills arrive when they are ready.
