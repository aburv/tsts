Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug? +

Fill out the form below with a detailed description of the issue, including steps to reproduce it. We'll investigate and respond to your email within 24-48 hours.

How long does it take to get a response? +

We typically respond to support requests within 24-48 hours during business days. Critical issues may be prioritized and addressed sooner.

What information should I include in my report? +

Please include: device type, operating system, app version, clear description of the issue, steps to reproduce, and screenshots if applicable. The more details, the faster we can help!

Can I request a feature? +

Absolutely! Use the form below and select "Feature Request" as the category. We'd love to hear your suggestions for improving Takbuff!
